## Day 3: Wednesday June 4

After two intense days of deep focus and fast shipping, today started at a different pace. We took a well-earned break to step away from our laptops and explore the city that's been hosting our hackathon week – **Nairobi**.

---

### 1. Exploring the Heart of Nairobi

We began the day by meeting our local guide, **Eric**, along with his two colleagues. All three were born and raised in Kenya and shared incredible insights about the city and its history.

Eric walked us through the streets of central Nairobi, giving us a personal look into the country's rich culture and stories.

Some standout moments:

- 🏛️ Learning about **Jomo Kenyatta**, the first president of Kenya who led the country to independence  
- 🔥 Hearing about **Dedan Kimathi**, a key leader of the Mau Mau uprising  
- 💸 Visiting national monuments and statues featured on the KES banknotes  

It was a powerful way to connect with the spirit of the city.

---

### 2. A View from the Top

We headed next to the **Kenyatta International Conference Centre (KICC)** for a rooftop view of Nairobi.

From the top, we were lucky enough to spot:

- 🏔️ **Mount Kenya** peeking through the haze  
- 🌄 **Ngong Hills** rolling to the west  
- 🌲 The **Aberdare Range** in the distance  
- 🦓 Even a glimpse of **Nairobi National Park**  

The view gave us a whole new perspective on where we've been working from this week – wild, busy, and full of energy.

---

### 3. Nairobi's Newest Celebrity

One of the most joyful moments of the day came when a group of local schoolchildren suddenly ran over to **Vincent**, treating him like a full-blown Hollywood celebrity.

Within seconds, he was surrounded by excited kids all trying to take photos with him. He handled the fame with grace and a lot of smiles 😎

---

### 4. Refuel and Reset

We wrapped up our city adventure with a long, relaxed lunch at a 5-star hotel in the centre of Nairobi.

- A beautiful buffet 🌽  
- Cold, fresh drinks 🍹  
- Quiet time to rest and reset together as a team  

---

### 5. Back to Build Mode

After lunch, we headed back to HQ to dive gently back into hackathon work. There were a few bug fixes, sync-ups, and planning sessions for tomorrow.

Sometimes the best way to move forward is to step away for a moment. Today gave us the space to reflect, recharge, and get ready for what's next.

---

Back to building! 

*The Tracer Team*
